Try it on your own files

tamperlint runs on your machine: no GPU, no API keys, and your documents never leave your computer.

pip install "tamperlint[synth] @ git+$repo"
tamperlint check statement.pdf
tamperlint check statement.pdf -f html -o reports/
Every document on this page is synthetic, issued by a fictional institution and marked SPECIMEN. Findings are evidence for a human reviewer, not proof of fraud. tamperlint is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence$build.
